Glossary

WRS / DRSWRS is a separate 0–100 diagnostic. DRS 3.00.00 uses per-level vector ratios and independently archived evidence; missing scenarios produce partial or indeterminate coverage. No certification is issued.
RiskLevelLow availability held & WRS ≥ 80 · Medium partial degradation or WRS 55–79 · Critical target unavailable or WRS < 55.
MitigationHeld ≈100% available · Partial some legitimate traffic lost · Failed auto-killed (target down ≥30 s) · Aborted manual stop.
TTD / TTMTime-To-Detect / Time-To-Mitigate — populated from the run when a protection source or auto-kill timing is observed.
RPSRequests per second (Layer 7). cfg→eff = configured cap → effective rate the target sustained; a large gap means the protection rate-limited or tarpitted the flood. Volumetric PPS is out of scope.

Automatically generated resilience evidence. Bounded HTTP generator — no volumetric, amplification or spoofing vector. WEDOS internal use.
