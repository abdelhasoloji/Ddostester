Glossary

WRS / DRSWRS is a separate 0–100 diagnostic. DRS 3.00.00 uses per-level vector ratios and independently archived evidence; missing scenarios produce partial or indeterminate coverage. No certification is issued.
RiskLevelLow availability held & WRS ≥ 80 · Medium partial degradation or WRS 55–79 · Critical probe-failure threshold or WRS < 55. Unknown = insufficient evidence; no risk conclusion.
Diagnostic verdictHeld ≈100% available · Partial some legitimate traffic lost · Failed probe-failure criteria or automatic stop · Aborted manual stop.
TTD / TTMTime-To-Detect / Time-To-Mitigate — supplied protection telemetry; an automatic-stop timestamp is not a mitigation timestamp.
RPSRequests per second (Layer 7). cfg→eff = configured cap → mean rate reported by the generator; a gap alone cannot identify filtering, target capacity or a generator bottleneck. Volumetric PPS is out of scope.

Automatically generated resilience evidence. HTTP observations and synthetic network-bench evidence are separate scopes; missing evidence is not a success. WEDOS internal use.
