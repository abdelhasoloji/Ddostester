Glossary

Verdict

heldLegitimate availability held (≥99%) at the delivered rate — a diagnostic at the delivered load; continuous availability and a DRS score are not established.
partialSome legitimate impact — availability dipped below 99% while the target stayed up.
breachedThe run met the probe-failure or automatic-stop criteria; probe sample success does not directly measure outage duration. Completion and resilience verdict are separate; a breach does not imply an automatic kill.
inconclusiveThe measurement can't prove resilience: the vector under-delivered (achieved ≪ configured rate), the generator was degraded, the probe was contended, or the run was aborted. Not a pass.

Mechanism

servedSuccessful HTTP responses observed by the generator; origin and edge attribution require independent telemetry.
rejected / tarpittedHTTP failures or transport failures observed by the generator. These alone do not establish filtering or deliberate tarpitting.
shedFor slow-connection vectors: slow/incomplete connections ended; the cause requires independent telemetry.
blockedGenerator failures observed; their location and cause are unresolved.
